FEATURED PRODUCT

NexaRank

Context-aware headless CMS & merchandising control plane

NexaRank sits between search engines and storefronts to control product discovery using AI and rule-based intelligence. Unlike generic headless CMS platforms that deliver static content, NexaRank evaluates real-time page context, search signals, and business rules to return the right content for the right moment — the open source alternative to Endeca Experience Manager.

  • Product ranking control (boost, pin, bury)
  • Merchandising rules engine
  • AI-assisted optimization
  • Search performance feedback loops
